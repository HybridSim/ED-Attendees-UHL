Background: The MIDCARE operational model provides a dynamic, site-specific representation of emergency care demand, trolley pressure and capacity at University Hospital Limerick (UHL), complementing ESRI’s regional projections.

Methods: Historic ED attendances 2014-2025 are projected forward using compound growth. Trolley demand is derived from attendances, conversion to admission and length of stay. Capacity growth is modelled separately. Critical-mass breach years are detected automatically.

Gaps filled relative to ESRI: ESRI projections are regional, scenario-based and non-operational. This model is site-level, dynamic and interactive, displaying congestion and critical thresholds not measured in HIQA/ESRI frameworks.

Results: The dashboard shows time paths of demand, capacity and shortfall and reveals how small growth variations produce large overcrowding effects over time.

Conclusions: The model operationalises transparency recommendations by making assumptions explicit and testable in real time.
